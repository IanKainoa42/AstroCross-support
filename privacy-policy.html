Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Summary: AstroCross does not collect personal data for the developer. Race settings stay on your device. Optional online play uses Apple Game Center.

What AstroCross stores on your device

  • Race tuning and control preferences (for example Large Controls, Left-Handed, haptics)
  • Local game progress related to racing on this device

This data is saved using your device’s local storage.

What AstroCross does NOT collect

  • No developer-run analytics or advertising identifiers
  • No account created by AstroCross (no email/password sign-in in the app)
  • No location data collected by AstroCross
  • No crash reporting SDK operated by the developer

Game Center

If you choose to play online or use leaderboards, AstroCross uses Apple Game Center. Game Center is operated by Apple and subject to Apple’s privacy policy. AstroCross does not receive a separate developer analytics feed from Game Center beyond what Apple provides for multiplayer and leaderboards.

Children’s privacy

AstroCross does not collect personal information from children for the developer. Online features require Game Center as provided by Apple.

Data deletion

Delete the app to remove local preferences and on-device data. Game Center data is managed through your Apple ID / Game Center settings.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
