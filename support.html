AstroCross Support

Getting started

AstroCross is a zero-gravity arcade racer. There is no grip and no gravity — you fly a ship with thrust and torque around short, fat circuits, race bots or friends on Game Center, and chase best-lap times on each track.

Tips

  • Garage: Pick a track, set the field, then start a solo race
  • Controls: Thumb pads on the sides (or a keyboard on Mac Catalyst / with a keyboard connected). Large Controls and Left-Handed options are in settings
  • Online: Sign in to Game Center for Quick Match and invites — “Race me in AstroCross”
  • Leaderboards: Best lap per track (elapsed time) and all-time race wins

Tracks

Ian’s Maze, Omega, Crossover, Serpent, Oval, Wedge, and Kidney.
